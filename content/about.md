I started on community, moved through the app, and tech-led activities before leading Runna's work in Strava Experience. I've since moved to platform, leading app infrastructure. Day to day I work across TypeScript, React Native, AWS, and GraphQL: product features and the platform they run on.

Before Runna I was at PwC, first through the Flying Start degree apprenticeship with the University of Leeds (Computer Science, First Class) and then as an associate. I started building software at school, including student-run tools at Wellington College. Those earlier projects are below.

I care about products that help people, hard problems, and owning the standard of the work.
